Hi! I'm Geoffrey.

Welcome! I teach 9th grade ELA and 9th through 12th grade multilingual learners at Hamtramck High School in metro Detroit. With a background that blends English literature and 25 years in technology, I bring a unique perspective to today's classroom challenges.

I believe every student has something amazing to offer. My teaching focuses on curiosity and creativity—I want my students to explore, question, and discover their own paths to learning.

My Journey

Once upon a time (after earning my first MA), I took a detour from academia to explore a career in computing. That path served me well for many years, but life has a way of guiding us back to our true passions. By 2020, my enthusiasm for computing had waned, and the COVID-19 pandemic prompted some serious soul-searching. When I realized I wasn't finding joy in my work anymore, I decided to pivot back to my original love—teaching English.

Looking Forward

These days, I'm researching how tabletop roleplaying games can enhance literacy education. There's something magical about creating learning environments where students actively participate in their educational journey rather than passively consuming information. As I wrap up my MAT at Wayne State University, I'm constantly exploring the sweet spot between technology use and experiential learning. My goal is to develop teaching methods that honor traditional literacy skills while preparing students for our increasingly digital world.
