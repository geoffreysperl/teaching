## Professional Journey

As mentioned in my About page, my path to education has been non-linear. After earning my first MA, I moved into computing rather than pursuing a doctorate in English. I worked in technology for 25 years, gaining experience that now informs my approach to education. By 2020, I realized I was no longer enjoying computing, and the COVID-19 pandemic prompted me to reassess my career path and personal happiness. This reflection led me back to my original interest—teaching English.

## Current Position

I am a full-time English Language Arts and ELL teacher at Hamtramck High School in metro Detroit. I also teach ESL at Wayne County Community College District and have experience as a long-term substitute at Eastpointe Middle School.

## Education

I am currently pursuing a Master of Arts in Teaching at Wayne State University, focusing on secondary ELA with an ELL endorsement.

## Research Interests

My current research focuses on the effects of tabletop roleplaying games (TTRPGs) in the classroom on the academic literacy of secondary students. This work examines how structured roleplay can enhance student engagement and literacy development through:

* Reading comprehension and critical analysis
* Vocabulary development and language skills
* Writing skills and creative expression
* Narrative competence and storytelling

## Teaching Innovations

I've developed several innovative teaching approaches that have proven effective in my classrooms:

### Character Analysis Role-Playing (CARP)
During my student teaching in 2023-24, I developed and implemented a lesson using concepts from tabletop role-playing games to analyze characters in "The Most Dangerous Game." By adapting a solo TTRPG called "The Last Tea Shop Complete," I created an engaging approach to character analysis where students interviewed potential heroes or villains. This innovative method achieved 100% participation and resulted in creative audio recordings from students.

### Wakanda Gallery Walk
At Eastpointe Middle School, I created a culturally responsive assessment inspired by Marvel's Black Panther. This creative approach connected geographical concepts to students' interests and improved assessment outcomes while deepening cultural understanding.
