I'm a dad, husband, educator, storyteller, writer, and podcaster.

A long time ago, I graduated with both a BA and MA in English... but I then chased a career in IT. The COVID-19 pandemic made me question my career and if I was happy. I wasn't. After weighing my options, I pivoted back to my original interest—teaching English.
