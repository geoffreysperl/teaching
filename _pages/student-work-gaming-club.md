In December of 2024, before the winter break, we held a special "fun day" at Hamtramck High School where I organized a board game activity to gauge student interest in gaming. The response was overwhelmingly positive, with students demonstrating enthusiasm for games that challenge their critical thinking, foster communication, and build community.

Based on this successful trial run and continued student interest, I'm excited to announce the launch of our after-school gaming club beginning in April 2025.

### Club Goals

* Develop literacy, strategic thinking, and decision-making skills
* Foster positive social interaction and communication
* Create a safe, inclusive space for all students
* Introduce students to diverse game mechanics and concepts
* Build community across grade levels and social groups

### Games & Activities

The club will feature various types of tabletop games, including:

* Roleplaying games
* Strategy board games
* Cooperative games
* Card games
* Social deduction games
* Abstract games

### Educational Benefits

While the primary purpose of the gaming club is to provide a fun, engaging after-school activity, the educational benefits are substantial:

* **Literacy Building**: Many games, especially roleplaying games, require the building of literacy to play the game
* **Critical Thinking**: Games require planning, resource management, and spatial reasoning
* **Mathematical Thinking**: Many games incorporate probability, statistics, and numerical reasoning
* **Communication Skills**: Games foster verbal negotiation and clear communication
* **Social-Emotional Learning**: Students practice good sportsmanship, patience, and collaboration
