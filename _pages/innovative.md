## Innovation in Education

Effective urban educators are innovative practitioners who can demonstrate the ability to solve problems, develop ideas, and use creative methods. As an innovative practitioner, I believe in building upon existing ideas and methods, introducing new concepts to a foundation that has come before.

## My Innovation Process

My approach to innovation follows these principles:

1. **Identify authentic challenges** - Recognize real barriers to student engagement or comprehension
2. **Research existing solutions** - Investigate both traditional and novel approaches
3. **Adapt and combine methods** - Build on proven strategies while incorporating creative elements
4. **Implement with flexibility** - Introduce innovations while remaining responsive to feedback
5. **Evaluate effectiveness** - Assess impact on engagement, comprehension, and skill development

## Innovation in Action: Character Analysis Role-Playing (CARP)

My Character Analysis Role-Playing (CARP) lesson exemplifies my approach to innovation. Traditional character analysis often fails to engage students deeply with texts. By adapting concepts from tabletop role-playing games, I created an approach that transformed passive analysis into active engagement.

The innovation process for CARP included:
- Identifying limitations in traditional character analysis approaches
- Researching game-based learning principles
- Adapting a solo TTRPG called "The Last Tea Shop Complete" to educational purposes
- Carefully structuring the activity to align with learning standards
- Developing a creative assessment component through audio recordings

This innovative approach achieved 100% participation and yielded insightful character analyses from students who typically struggled with traditional literary analysis.

## Evidence-Based Innovation

My innovations are grounded in research on engagement, literacy development, and game-based learning. My thesis work on "The Effects of Playing Tabletop Roleplaying Games in the Classroom on the Academic Literacy of Secondary Students" informs my innovative teaching practices with theoretical understanding and evidence-based strategies.

By connecting classroom innovations to scholarly research, I ensure that creative approaches serve substantive learning goals rather than merely entertaining students.
